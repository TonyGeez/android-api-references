Extended [Adapter](../android/widget/Adapter.md) that is the bridge between a [android.widget.Spinner](../android/widget/Spinner.md) and its data. A spinner adapter allows to define two different views: one that shows the data in the spinner itself and one that shows the data in the drop down list when the spinner is pressed.
