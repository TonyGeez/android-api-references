An exception thrown by [android.app.appsearch.AppSearchSession](https://developer.android.com/reference/kotlin/android/app/appsearch/AppSearchSession.html#) or a subcomponent.

These exceptions can be converted into a failed [AppSearchResult](https://developer.android.com/reference/kotlin/android/app/appsearch/AppSearchResult.html#) for propagating to the client.
