A template for a [Control](https://developer.android.com/reference/kotlin/android/service/controls/Control.html#) with a single button that can be toggled between two states. The states for the toggle correspond to the states in [ControlButton.isChecked()](https://developer.android.com/reference/kotlin/android/service/controls/templates/ControlButton#isChecked()). An action on this template will originate a [BooleanAction](https://developer.android.com/reference/kotlin/android/service/controls/actions/BooleanAction.html#) to change that state.
