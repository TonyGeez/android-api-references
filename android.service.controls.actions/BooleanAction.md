Action sent by user toggling a [Control](https://developer.android.com/reference/kotlin/android/service/controls/Control.html#) between checked/unchecked. This action is available when the [Control](https://developer.android.com/reference/kotlin/android/service/controls/Control.html#) was constructed with either a [ToggleTemplate](https://developer.android.com/reference/kotlin/android/service/controls/templates/ToggleTemplate.html#) or a [ToggleRangeTemplate](https://developer.android.com/reference/kotlin/android/service/controls/templates/ToggleRangeTemplate.html#).
